# Modular and integral spectral algebras of abelian Cayley graphs

For a Cayley graph Γ = Cay(G, S) on a finite abelian group G and a commutative ring R, the spectral
algebra 𝒜_R(Γ) = R[A] is the algebra generated by the adjacency matrix A. Over a field whose
characteristic does not divide |G| it is a product of fields, one for each Galois orbit of
eigenvalues (Bhattacharjee, Mandal and Bhattacharya, *Spectral algebras of abelian Cayley graphs*,
[arXiv:2609.09222](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09222), Theorem 6.1). This paper treats the two cases
left outside that theorem, characteristic p dividing |G| and the integral algebra ℤ[A], and answers
two questions about the rational algebra raised in arXiv:2609.09222.

| | Statement |
|---|---|
| Theorem A | In characteristic p, with q a power of p divisible by the exponent of G_p and ≡ 1 modulo the exponent of G_{p′}: A^q is the adjacency matrix of Cay(G, π(S)), A − A^q is nilpotent, J(𝒜_K(Γ)) = (A − A^q)𝒜_K(Γ), and the quotient is 𝒜_K of the shadow Cay(G_{p′}, π(S)). So 𝒜_K(Γ) is semisimple iff every element of S has order prime to p. |
| Theorem B | For n = p^a m ≥ 3, p ∤ m, q = p^a, the minimal polynomial of A(C_n) in characteristic p is ∏_{d∣m} Ψ̄_d^{e_d}, with e_d = q for d ≥ 3 and ⌈q/2⌉ for d ≤ 2. Its degree is ⌊n/2⌋ + 1 for odd p and n/2 for p = 2. |
| Theorem C | The index I(n) of ℤ[A(C_n)] in the maximal order ∏_{d∣n} 𝒪_{ℚ(ζ_d)^+} is ∏ p^{2φ(d)/(ε_d ε_d′)} over d ∣ d′ ∣ n with d′/d a power of a prime p. It is never 1 for n ≥ 3, and v_p(I(n)) has a closed form. ℤ[A(C_n)] has index gcd(2, n) in the symmetric integer circulants. |
| Theorem D | The prism C_m □ K_2 has (N + t)/2 distinct eigenvalues, the most an abelian group of order N = 2m with t elements of order ≤ 2 allows, iff 4 ∤ m and 6 ∤ m. So noncyclic groups can attain the bound (arXiv:2609.09222, Remark 21.4), and the cyclic value ⌊N/2⌋ + 1 is not an upper bound for all groups (Remark 19.4). |

Here Ψ_d is the minimal polynomial of 2cos(2π/d), ε_d = 1 for d ≤ 2 and 2 for d ≥ 3. For example
I(30) = 2⁹·3⁶·5⁴, and the decagonal prism has 12 distinct eigenvalues on a group of order 20.
Section 6 and the footnotes correct several statements of arXiv:2609.09222 about isomorphism types
and the dimension bound.

## What is proved, and how

The general theorems are proved by hand in the paper (`preprint/main.tex`, built into `dist/`). The
Lean 4 development (`lean/`, no Mathlib, Lean v4.34.1) certifies every finite instance the paper
quotes, by evaluation in the kernel (`decide +kernel`, no `native_decide`):

| Statement | Lean theorems |
|---|---|
| ∏_{d∣n} Ψ_d is the minimal polynomial of A(C_n) over ℚ, 3 ≤ n ≤ 30 (annihilation plus a triangular rank certificate) | `Spectral.minpoly_Q_3` … `minpoly_Q_30` |
| Theorem B over 𝔽_p, p ≤ 7, p ∣ n ≤ 30 | `minpoly_F2_*`, `minpoly_F3_*`, `minpoly_F5_*`, `minpoly_F7_*` |
| Example 3.2 over 𝔽_3 | `matching_F3`, `triangles_F3` |
| Table 1 for n ≤ 16 as determinants of ℤ[A(C_n)] → 𝒪_n; Theorem C(i) for n ≤ 30; C(ii) for n ≤ 60 | `index_*`, `formula_*`, `table1_formula`, `valuation_formula` |
| Theorem D for 3 ≤ m ≤ 30, and the decagonal prism | `prism_*`, `prism_layout`, `decagonal_prism` |
| Example 6.1, the two algebras on ℤ/24 | `z24_S`, `z24_S'` |

`lean/Check.lean` prints the axioms of all 126 theorems; `scripts/audit_axioms.py` checks that only
the standard axioms occur. In fact 124 use `propext` alone and two use no axioms
(`verification/axioms.txt`).

Independent checks outside Lean (`scripts/run_all.sh`, PARI/GP and Python 3):

- `verification/gp/thmA.gp`: Theorem A on twelve groups of order at most 12 in characteristics 2, 3
  and 5, every connection set for |G| ≤ 8 and every symmetric one beyond (2412 cases): the shadow,
  the dimension of the radical and the semisimplicity criterion.
- `verification/gp/thmB.gp`: Theorem B for p ≤ 7 and p ∣ n ≤ 80, both as a product over pairs of
  roots of unity and as printed.
- `verification/gp/thmC.gp`: Theorem C(i) against discriminants for n ≤ 150, Lemma 4.3 for
  d < d′ ≤ 120, the field discriminants for d ≤ 150, and C(ii) for n ≤ 400.
- `verification/gp/sym.gp`: Proposition 4.1 by Smith normal form, n ≤ 40.
- `verification/gp/thmD.gp`: Theorem D exactly for m ≤ 60 and numerically for m ≤ 300.
- `verification/python/semisimple.py`: Theorem A again, without PARI.
- `verification/python/check_paper.py`: every number printed in the paper (both tables, the
  footnotes, Example 6.1, the decagonal prism, Proposition 5.2 for k = 2).
- `verification/python/check_lean_tables.py`: the data tables written into the Lean files (the
  polynomials Ψ_d and Table 1) agree with PARI/GP and with the paper.

## Layout

```
lean/                       Lean 4 (v4.34.1, no Mathlib)
  SpectralAlgebras/         Basic (lists, polynomials, determinants, ranks), Cycles, Index, Prism
  Check.lean                axiom audit
verification/gp/            thmA.gp, thmB.gp, thmC.gp, sym.gp, thmD.gp, psi.gp, common.gp
verification/python/        semisimple.py, check_paper.py, check_lean_tables.py
verification/axioms.txt     output of Check.lean
preprint/                   the paper (amsart) and its TikZ figures
dist/                       the paper as PDF, as LaTeX with PNG figures, and as an arXiv tarball
scripts/                    gen_bars.py, build_paper.sh, run_all.sh, audit_axioms.py
PRIORITY-CHECK.md           the dated check that the results were new
```

To reproduce: `scripts/run_all.sh` (PARI/GP, Python 3), `cd lean && lake build && lake env lean
Check.lean`, and `scripts/build_paper.sh` (TeX Live). The workflow `.github/workflows/verify.yml`
runs all three on every pull request.
